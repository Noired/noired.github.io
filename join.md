# Join the group

In January 2027 I will start my research group at the [CISPA Helmholtz Center for Information Security](https://cispa.de/) in Saarbrücken, Germany. I am looking for a PhD student and a postdoc to join me as early as March 2027. Applications are open now; I am planning to start interviews in November.

## Who am I looking for?

Above all, I am looking for <i>curious</i> people who like both careful thinking and getting things to work in practice. I aim to hire people who can work <i>independently</i> and also enjoy working <i>in a team</i>: people who are <i>responsible</i>, <i>open-minded</i> and <i>considerate</i> of others.

Technically speaking, the following are ***required***:
- Solid foundations in mathematics and machine learning
- Good coding skills and care for clean, well-structured code
- (**PhD student**) A Master's degree in computer science, mathematics, physics or a related field; completed or close to completion
- (**Postdoc**) A PhD in machine learning, AI or a related field (completed or close to completion), with a strong research record
- (**Postdoc**) The ability to drive research projects while working closely with the group, including mentoring students

While the following are ***nice to have***  :
- Experience with any of: geometric deep learning, learning on graphs, mechanistic interpretability, AI safety, or the internals of language models more generally
- Familiarity with the concepts and mathematics of symmetries, equivariance, and graphs
- (**PhD student**) A peer-reviewed publication, or other research experience such as a research-oriented thesis
- (**Postdoc**) Ideas for a research direction of your own you would like to pursue

## What to expect

**You will be joining a new lab.** As one of the first members of the group, you will get close one-to-one supervision early on, the chance to mentor interns and new students later, and the opportunity to help shape the lab's research direction and values.

**You will get a full-time, fully funded position at CISPA.** This comes with a competitive salary and the benefits of a German employment contract. You will also have access to CISPA's compute infrastructure and support for travelling to conferences.

**You will live and work in an international environment.** CISPA is a diverse, international workplace that operates fully in English. Saarbrücken is a university city close to the French border, with a campus shared by Saarland University and several computer science research institutes (and the cost of living is moderate compared with larger German cities 😉).

You can read more about working at CISPA [here](https://cispa.de/en/career).

## How to apply

Send me an email at *fabriziof (at) campus.technion.ac.il* with the subject "PhD application – [Your Name]" or "Postdoc application – [Your Name]", attaching a single PDF with:
- Your up-to-date CV;
- A note on (i) why you would like to pursue a PhD/Postdoc (as applicable); (ii) which research directions interest you; (iii) why you would like to join my group;
- One piece of work you are proud of (a paper, thesis or project; a link is fine), with a few lines on what you did and why it matters;
- The names and contact details of two referees, whom I will only contact for shortlisted candidates;
- For PhD applicants, your transcripts.

I review applications as they arrive. Those received by 15 November 2026 will receive full consideration, and interviews will start shortly after. Later applications are welcome for as long as the positions are open.

Formal applications go through CISPA’s application system, and I will point you to the next steps once we have been in touch.
